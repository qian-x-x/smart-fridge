## 最终生成提示词

Use case: product-mockup. Create ONE high-resolution photorealistic product photograph, an original unbranded household refrigerator design grounded in real French-door refrigerators and physically plausible internal construction. Intended as a realistic reference for rebuilding a web application's 3D fridge, not an app screenshot. Single continuous image, landscape 4:3 composition, whole appliance and both opened doors fully visible with generous breathing room. A full-size approximately 185 cm tall, 90 cm wide refrigerator in brushed stainless steel, realistic restrained industrial design, gently radiused sheet-metal corners, substantial insulated door thickness, discreet recessed handles, realistic seams and hinge covers, small adjustable feet. Upper French doors both open about 105 degrees, showing their molded white inner liners, continuous gray magnetic gasket seals, and three transparent door bins on each door, with the lowest door bins deep enough for milk bottles. A physically credible center sealing mullion attached to one upper door, no permanent central pillar dividing the fresh-food cavity. Main fresh-food cavity is one continuous white thermoformed liner with rounded interior corners; rear air circulation slots; shelf mounting rails on side walls; three adjustable tempered glass shelves with thin aluminum front trims, two side-by-side transparent humidity crisper drawers at the bottom of the main cavity, realistic drawer supports. Below the upper compartment, a separate full-width temperature-controlled drawer pulled forward 20 cm showing its low divided tray; at bottom a deep freezer drawer pulled forward 40 cm showing its sliding upper basket and lower divided storage basket, realistic telescopic slides and thick insulated fronts. Components must be mechanically coherent and nonintersecting; lower drawers must fit into the cabinet, no floating shelves, no phantom doors, no impossible hinge geometry. Sparse realistic groceries to establish scale without obscuring construction: a glass milk bottle and a small egg carton on glass shelves, a few vegetables inside crispers, jars and a tall bottle in door bins, two neatly wrapped frozen packs in freezer baskets. No room-temperature pantry inside the refrigerated cabinet. Soft neutral white interior LED strips along the inner sidewalls, realistic refraction in clear plastics, visible glass thickness, subtle brushed metal anisotropy, natural soft shadows and believable depth. Professional architectural product photography, 50 mm lens, camera near frontal at chest height, just a subtle right-side three-quarter view so cabinet depth and door thickness are legible, straight verticals, physically correct proportions, sharp detailed materials, high dynamic range without overprocessing. Quiet cool light gray studio cyclorama background and matte gray floor, no kitchen decor. Photographic realism instead of a stylized toy-like 3D render. No UI, no interface cards, no callouts, no arrows, no labels, no text, no logo, no brand mark, no watermark, no collage, no cutaway walls. Output a polished photographic concept of one real-looking manufactured appliance.
